IEEE MTT-S Introduction

What is IEEE MTT-S?

The IEEE Microwave Theory and Techniques Society (MTT-S) is a professional organization dedicated to advancing the theory and applications of microwaves and related electromagnetic phenomena.

MTT-S focuses on:

  • Microwave engineering and circuit design
  • Antenna theory and design
  • Electromagnetic wave propagation
  • RF and wireless technologies
  • Millimeter-wave and terahertz applications
  • Computational electromagnetics

About Our Chapter

Our Mission

The IEEE MTT-S Chapter at MITS-DU aims to foster innovation and excellence in microwave engineering, antenna design, and electromagnetic applications among students and professionals in Gwalior.

What We Offer

We organize technical workshops, seminars, hands-on training sessions, and networking events to bridge the gap between academic learning and industry practices in microwave and RF technologies.

Community Impact

Our chapter serves as a hub for researchers, engineers, and students passionate about microwave technology, fostering collaboration and knowledge sharing across institutions.

Global Connection

As part of IEEE, we connect with a global network of professionals, providing access to conferences, publications, and collaborative opportunities worldwide.
